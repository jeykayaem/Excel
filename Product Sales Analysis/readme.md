# Product Sales Analysis

An Excel analysis of a hypothetical retail sales dataset for four products (P1–P4). The workbook includes the data and analysis for the five questions below; a separate `data.csv` file is not required.

## Questions explored

1. Monthly sales patterns across the four products.
2. Which product had the highest sales over the period.
3. Estimated units sold on December 31 if stores had been open.
4. The potential impact of discontinuing one product.
5. A 2024 sales and revenue forecast.

## Workbook

Open `Product Sales Analysis.xls` in Microsoft Excel. Worksheets `Q1`–`Q5` contain the corresponding analyses, and `Product Sales Analysis` contains supporting data.

The source workbook is in the legacy Excel 97–2003 `.xls` format. The analyses are educational and rely on a hypothetical dataset; forecasts and estimates are not guarantees.

## Data and credits

The dataset represents monthly product unit sales and revenue from 2010 through 2023. The original project description credits Jashanpreet Sangar for creating and sharing the hypothetical dataset.
